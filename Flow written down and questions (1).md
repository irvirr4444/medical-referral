# **Referral → Scheduling → Visit Workflow**

## 

## **1\. Referral Intake**

* **Referral arrives** — email or fax (PDF attached) Sources: SNF · ALF · hospital · home health · physician office · self-referral*.* **Ultimately referral arrives at infobox\***  
* **Open PDF, extract the “7 required fields”.**   
  **Patient name**  
  **Date of birth**  
  **Contact number**  
  **Patient address**  
  **Home health or hospice agency**  
  **Wound/clinical information**  
  **Insurance information**  
* **Call referral partner** (every referral): thank · confirm · request anything missing. **Minimum threshold to proceed: name, DOB, phone, address**  
  **If missing: escalate to assigned marketer for follow-up**  
  **If threshold met but incomplete: send to case manager, follow up on gaps.**  
* **Referral partner reachable?**   
  * **No →** Forward PDF to the source's assigned Marketer to follow up (address unverified \= can't schedule) → proceeds to Step 5  
  * **Yes →** proceeds to Step 5

\*Info box team: 3 primary agents (Braxton \+ 2 others); email address: info@westcostwound.com

## **2\. Handoff & Assignment**

5. **Ready to hand off.**   
6. **Email referrals**: info box agent sends an acknowledgement to the source, CC the assigned case manager.\*   
7. Splits into two parallel actions:  
   * **Email the assigned case manager \+ PDF** (CM chosen by patient location)  
   * **Forward PDF to face sheet team** — build chart in DRK  
8. **Log info-box half of [monday.com](http://monday.com)**. The infobox agent fills patient information in [monday.com](http://monday.com) master sheet table and fills the ‘Sent By’ column.    
9. **Case manager owns the patient.** 

\*16 case managers, each assigned to a geographic area with dedicated providers. No formal list in [Monday.com](http://Monday.com); Braxton to provide the list.

\*\***Notify CM when ownership is written** (email: patient, area, short why, next step = provider, PDF when available). Monday Case Manager column change does **not** notify today — own this in our handoff. Details: [docs/GMAIL_ALERTS.md](docs/GMAIL_ALERTS.md).

## **3\. Provider Selection & Scheduling**

10. **CM selects the appropriate company provider** from their list of assigned providers in their area. \*  
11. **Provider available in that area?**  
    * **No →** (no provider assigned, or too far from territory) → Nicole reviews → **Discharge (DC)**  
    * **Yes →** continue  
12. **Provider confirms within 1 hour?**  
    * **No →** CM places patient where it best fits the provider's schedule → proceeds to Step 14  
    * **Yes →** Send Referral to provider.\*\*Schedule per provider's availability. Patient scheduled (24–48 hrs).  
13. **CM fills CM-half of monday.com \+ DRK.**

\*No formal list of the provider companies in [monday.com](http://monday.com).Additional: CMs use Routing software for provider scheduling. Both need to be provided.   
\*\*On the mastersheet table, “Referral sent to provider” column defines if the referral is sent to the provider or not.  
\*\*\*On the mastersheet table, "Appointment date” column displays when the appointment date is scheduled.

## **4\. End-of-Day Check**

15. **End of day: patient scheduled?\***  
    * **Blank →** Lead follows up with CM over Teams: why not scheduled  
      * **Resolvable by CM/lead?**  
        * **No →** Unresolved cases escalated to Nicole/upper management via email \+ a separate spreadsheet  
        * **Yes →** proceeds to Step 16  
    * **Scheduled →** proceeds to Step 16\*\*  
16. **The patient enters the weekly schedule.**

\*On the mastersheet table on [monday.com](http://monday.com), “Scheduled status” column defines if the patient is scheduled or not.  
\*\*On the mastersheet table on monday.com, “Scheduled complete” column defines if scheduling is complete or not. 

## **5\. Weekly Visit Cycle**

17. **Weekly visit on Masters \+ route mgmt. Estimated file size of step:**  
18. **Visit completed?** (DRK progress note)   
    **Not seen →** **Mark NOT seen, reschedule weekly**   
    * **3 weeks in a row not seen?**  
      * **Yes →** CM sends referral to upper mgmt for **Discharge (DC)** approval, reason: noncompliance  
        * **No →** loops back to Step 17 (Weekly visit) — *unless the patient is placed on hold, in which case they exit this loop and move to Step 20 (Patient on hold).*  
    * **Seen →** **Initial visit: mark SEEN on monday.com**

        
19. **Wound healed or patient expired?\***  
    * **Healed / expired →**  
      * Healed: provider → QA → discharge  
      * Expired: CM removes from schedule → DC approval  
    * **(neither) →** continue to Step 20  
20. **Patient on hold?** (hospital / vacation / other)  
    * **Yes →** **Move to holds team \+ holds list**  
      * **Ready to return?**  
        * **No →** loops back to holds list  
        * **Yes →** loops back into the weekly visit cycle (Step 17\)  
    * **No →** loops back into the weekly visit cycle (Step 17\)

\* On mastersheet table on [monday.com](http://monday.com), ‘Visit status’ column defines if  
a) wound is healed  
b) patient is being hospitalized  
c) patient is expired  
d) patient is on hold
